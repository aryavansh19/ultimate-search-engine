I let Instagram watch me for seven years. Then I read the file.

Instagram will hand you everything it recorded about you. I downloaded my own export — 209 MB across 1,057 files — and counted all of it. Every reel, every story, every save, every advertiser holding my name. Some of it I expected. One number made me put the laptop down.
